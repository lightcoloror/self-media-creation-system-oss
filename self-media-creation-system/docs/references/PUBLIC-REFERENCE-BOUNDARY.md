# Reference-card boundary

The CAST card contains first-party bibliographic/retrieval metadata for an
external official methodology reference. No DOCX, article body, example works or
third-party media are redistributed. Its original retrieval hashes and attribution
are historical and have not been regenerated or presented as current byte proof.

The official notification page was read during this public-source review; its
title and April 15/17, 2026 dates match the card. This checks page metadata, not
the complete attachment, the historical WCF archive hashes, present clinical
evidence, insurance terms, platform requirements or an actual content review.

The card is a methodology reference only. Each current scientific claim needs
its own admissible primary evidence, and real human reviews cannot be replaced
with the synthetic tests supplied here.
