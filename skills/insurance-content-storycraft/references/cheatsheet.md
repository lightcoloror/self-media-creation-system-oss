# Storycraft Cheatsheet

## Route

```text
verified/user-confirmed material
→ is there a consequential change?
  → no: direct answer / mechanism / case review / tool action
  → yes: identify goal → disruption → gap → pressure → choice → value shift
→ choose one medium adapter
→ preserve facts and judgment
→ run applicable quality and risk gates
→ human publication decision
```

## Four media

| Medium | Keep | Cut or avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short video | one change, immediate watch reason, action/reaction, brief boundary | slow background, empty suspense, complete biography |
| WeChat article | scene–explanation alternation, context, evidence, honest uncertainty | decorative detail, forced closure, scene after scene without interpretation |
| Interview | scene questions, exact-quote status, alternatives, action, aftermath | opinion-only prompts, scripted praise, inferred feelings |
| Personal brand | repeated choices, specific habits, admitted limits, learning over time | credential climax, self-labels, universal life lesson |

## Minimum evidence states

- `verified quote`: exact wording and authorization known
- `near-quote`: explicitly labeled paraphrase
- `observable fact`: source or fact ID attached
- `author reaction`: the creator's own confirmed feeling or judgment
- `agent hypothesis`: internal candidate only; never write as audience consensus

## Fail fast

- No evidence → do not invent.
- No change → do not force a story.
- No choice → consider process explanation.
- No boundary → do not generalize.
- No authorization → do not expose or publish.
