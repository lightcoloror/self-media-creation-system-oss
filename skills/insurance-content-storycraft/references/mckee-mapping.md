# McKee Mapping for Evidence-Bound Social Content

This mapping distils useful craft principles from the user's local McKee collections. It does not copy passages and does not turn feature-film structure into a compulsory social template.

| McKee concept | Operational use here | Safety adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Structure as selected events | Keep events that create causal change and express the judgment | Facts remain bound to the canonical fact card |
| Desire | Give the protagonist one immediate, observable goal | Do not invent hidden motives |
| Antagonistic forces | Identify resource, time, institutional, interpersonal, environmental, or internal pressure | Do not manufacture a villain or outrage target |
| Gap | Record the difference between expected and actual result | Both sides require evidence when presented as fact |
| Choice under pressure | Reveal character and service through action and tradeoff | Creator credit must pass service-causality review |
| Turning point | Change meaning, direction, stakes, or value state | Prefer small truthful changes over dramatic inflation |
| Crisis/climax | Place the consequential choice or revelation near the story close | Short video still previews an immediate watch reason |
| Resolution | Show the new state and remaining uncertainty | No general promise from one case |
| Dialogue as verbal action | Keep words that change the scene or expose pressure | No fabricated dialogue; preserve authorization and context |
| Subtext | Allow the audience to infer part of the meaning | Do not immediately explain every quote or emotion |

## Deliberately not imported

- mandatory three-act or feature-film page formulas;
- ever-rising drama regardless of evidence;
- invented thoughts, composite dialogue, or reconstructed scenes presented as real;
- trauma, illness, claim denial, or client vulnerability used as spectacle;
- the assumption that a strong story proves a general medical or insurance conclusion.

## Unified chain

```text
locked facts
→ route selection
→ story-design card when structure_route=story-arc
→ serious-content precheck when applicable
→ draft
→ initial-draft quality gate
→ human voice rewrite
→ post-freeze hook and serious-content review
→ final factual/privacy/compliance review
→ human publication decision
```
