# Cross-Media Storytelling

## Start with a story unit, not a format

Before deciding length or platform, identify the smallest truthful unit that changes meaning:

1. a person wants an observable result;
2. something disturbs the prior state;
3. reality differs from expectation;
4. pressure creates real alternatives;
5. a choice, discovery, reaction, or consequence changes the value state;
6. the audience can say what is different at the end.

For first-person material, also name the brief moment of realization or change. For reported nonfiction, the unit must be supported by observation, records, or an authorized interview. If there is only useful information and no consequential change, choose `direct-answer`, `mechanism-explanation`, `case-review`, or `tool-action` instead of manufacturing a story.

Use this internal card before adaptation:

```yaml
protagonist_goal: immediate and observable
scene_anchor: one fact-bound action, object, setting, record, or sensory detail
inciting_change: what disturbed the prior state
expectation_gap: expected versus actual
first_attempt_and_feedback: what was tried first and what reality returned
pressure_and_alternatives: what made the next action a choice
turning_point: action, reaction, revelation, reversal, or consequence
value_before_after: what meaning or condition changed
scene_evidence: action, object, setting, record, or authorized quote
five_second_moment: smallest truthful realization or transformation; optional for reported stories
explanation_claim: the one claim the story illustrates
boundary: what this individual story cannot prove
```

The canonical `story-design-card.v1` remains mandatory when `structure_route=story-arc`; this extraction card does not replace it.

## Short video

Use a compressed causal chain:

1. preview a result, choice conflict, or decisive scene within two seconds;
2. give only the minimum context needed to understand the gap;
3. move through pressure, action/reaction, and one to three truthful turning points;
4. place original audio or a short quote where it changes meaning, not as decoration;
5. state the central judgment near the close;
6. use two to four sentences for explanation and boundary.

Do not confuse withholding with suspense. The opening must provide a real reason to watch and the body must fulfill it. A 30–90 second script normally carries one central change, not a complete life history. One turn is enough when it completes that change; do not invent a second turn for structural neatness.

## WeChat article / longform

Use alternating scene and understanding:

1. open with a scene, consequential action, or question;
2. establish why this particular case matters;
3. return to reporting, evidence, mechanism, and context when the reader needs interpretation;
4. use small anecdotes as evidence or illustration inside the larger argument;
5. return to the person or scene after the explanation;
6. end with the earned change, remaining uncertainty, or next action.

Longform permits more context, not more decorative detail. Every scene, quote, statistic, and background passage must serve the destination. A true story may remain incomplete; do not force a resolution that reporting does not support.

## Interview and interview video

Before live elicitation or guide drafting, read [material-interview](../../../self-media-creation-system/skills/material-interview/SKILL.md). Reuse existing answers; ask only consequential gaps and stop when material is sufficient. The prompts below are an optional menu, not required slots or a fixed order. Unknown memories may remain unknown; do not force quotes, feelings, or a transformation. For viewpoint-led content, concrete support need not be a personal scene.

Interview for relevant events and evidence when they serve the selected story:

- “当时你在哪里？谁在场？”
- “你原来以为会怎样？后来发生了什么？”
- “哪一步最难？你当时有哪些选择？”
- “你还记得谁说了哪句话吗？这是原话还是大意？”
- “接下来你具体做了什么？结果怎样？还有什么不知道？”

Keep four layers separate: verified quotation, near-quote/paraphrase, observable fact, and editor inference. Ask permission for identifiable or sensitive use. Do not promise the interviewee a favorable portrayal. If a client audio line is not recorded, specify the question and selection rule instead of scripting a testimonial.

Build two explicit layers before editing:

- **Action layer:** where the person was, what changed, what they did, what happened next.
- **Meaning layer:** what they believed before, what the event changed, and what they understand now.

The action layer supplies the story spine. The meaning layer interprets it. Do not replace missing action with retrospective opinion.

For editing, choose clips that perform an action: introduce pressure, revise an expectation, reveal a decision, or mark a value shift. A vivid sentence that does none of these may still be atmosphere, but it should not carry the story spine.

## Personal brand

Let identity accumulate through repeated choices:

- capture one short daily moment instead of waiting for a dramatic biography;
- show what the person notices, chooses, refuses, changes, or admits under pressure;
- preserve contradictions and unfinished learning when they are true;
- use credentials as context, not as the climax;
- repeat a few values through different scenes rather than repeating self-descriptions.

A personal-brand story succeeds when the audience can infer a stable trait from action. If the trait disappears when the action is removed, the piece was self-praise rather than characterization.

Use the story-category field to distinguish origin, failure, customer transformation, principle, method-origin, and ordinary case moments. The category guides selection; it does not authorize a stock template or change who the truthful protagonist is.

## Advisory craft diagnostics

- Use “but/therefore” as a causal reading test, not as required wording. Equivalent causal connections are valid.
- Review `Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories`; a dimension may be not applicable, and no score can compensate for missing evidence.
- Run a causal trim pass: remove repetition and decorative setup while preserving the event → response → choice → consequence chain. Do not enforce a universal 20–30% deletion quota.

## Final cross-media checks

- Is this a causal change or only chronology?
- Is the scene supported, authorized, and necessary?
- Does the protagonist make or face a meaningful choice?
- Is the key quote performing an action?
- Can the audience name the before/after value shift?
- Does the explanation add context without flattening the story?
- Is the story illustrating a claim rather than pretending to prove a population-level conclusion?
- Did the platform adaptation preserve the same facts and judgment?
- Is the publication learning plan limited to one variable and explicitly noncausal?
